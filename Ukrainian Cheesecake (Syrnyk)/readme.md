# Ukrainian Cheesecake (Syrnyk)

## 1. Dough

### Ingredients:

- 500 g flour
- 250 g margarine (or butter)
- 150 g powdered sugar (or regular sugar)
- 2 eggs
- 2 heaping tablespoons of cocoa powder
- 1 packet of baking powder (18–20 g)

### Instructions:

1. Melt the margarine and let it cool slightly
2. Beat the sugar and eggs together thoroughly (both yolks and whites).
3. Mix the flour with the baking powder and cocoa powder.
4. Combine everything and knead into a dough. (I use food processor - dough blade)
5. Divide the dough into two equal portions.
6. Wrap each portion in plastic wrap and place in the freezer for 2–3 hours, until firm enough to grate.

## 2. Cheese Filling
### Ingredients:

- 300 g sugar
- 250 g butter, softened
- 1 kg cottage cheese (I use the kind from Costco – see picture). 
Traditionally, Ukrainian syrnyk is made with farmer's cheese (also called tvorog), which is drier and denser than North American cottage cheese. Farmer's cheese works well too. If using cottage cheese, drain it first if it's very wet, using a fine-mesh strainer or cheesecloth.
- 8 egg (separate yolks and whites)
- 2 tablespoons of starch (cornstarch or potato starch)
- 1 packet vanilla sugar (10 g), and lemon zest
- Optional: raisins and small pieces of canned apricots

### Instructions:

1. Grind the cottage cheese using a meat grinder (I use good processor - chopping blade); you can also use potato ricer or just masher.
2. Cream the butter with the sugar and egg yolks.
3. Combine the cheese mixture (step 1) with the butter mixture (step 2).
4. Add the starch and mix well.
5. Beat the 8 egg whites until stiff peaks form. (Egg whites whip better when chilled; refrigerate them for 10–15 minutes beforehand.)
6. Finally, add the vanilla sugar and beaten egg whites (and raising/dry apricot pieces if you wish). Gently fold everything together until smooth and evenly combined, being careful not to deflate the egg whites.

## 3. Assembling and Baking the Cheesecake

### Baking pan:  
approx. 8 × 12 inches

### Instructions:

1. Line the baking pan with parchment paper and lightly grease it with oil.
3. Remove dough from the freezer. Using the large-hole side of a box grater (coarse cheese grater), grate one portion of dough evenly over the bottom of the baking pan.
4. Pour the cheese filling over the grated dough and spread it evenly.
5. Grate the second portion of frozen dough evenly over the top of the cheese filling.
6. Bake for approximately 1 hour at 360F.
7. Test for doneness by inserting a wooden toothpick or matchstick into the cheesecake. If it comes out clean, the cheesecake is ready.
8. Let the cheesecake cool completely before cutting. The filling will firm up as it cools.

### Enjoy!
